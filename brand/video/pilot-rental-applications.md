# Pilot video: rental applications (about 45 to 50 seconds)

Format: vertical 9:16 for Reels and Shorts, or square 1:1 for Facebook and LinkedIn. Record once at the largest size; crop later.
Voice: stock Australian voice for the pilot (own-voice clone later). Captions burned in, because most people watch on mute.

## Narration (106 words), one block per scene so each can be re-recorded alone

**Scene 1 (0 to 8 s)**
Twenty groups through your rental on Saturday. By Sunday night, you're sorting paper forms and typing details into a spreadsheet.

**Scene 2 (8 to 22 s)**
With Open Home Hero, every visitor scans the QR code on your flyer, or taps the link in your listing. The application opens on their phone. No app. No account.

**Scene 3 (22 to 34 s)**
The form collects all the relevant information from each applicant. Then they hit submit.

**Scene 4 (34 to 44 s)**
Back at your desk, every application is in one place, organised by property. No chasing. No re-typing.

**Scene 5 (44 to 50 s)**
Try Open Home Hero free for fourteen days at openhomehero dot com dot au.

(Scene 1 is a picture of the problem, not a customer claim. Do not say "22 groups" or any other figure as fact.)

## Shot list: what to record on screen, sample data only

Use a test listing with a made-up address (for example 24 Grandview Terrace) and a made-up applicant ("Sample Applicant", a fake email). Never use a real person's details. Delete the test listing afterwards.

| Scene | On screen |
|---|---|
| 1 | A plain navy title card with the words "Saturday: 20 groups through. Sunday: sorting forms." (made in the brand style, no recording needed) |
| 2 | The listing page with the QR code and the Copy buttons; then a phone (or the browser at phone width) opening the rental form from the link |
| 3 | The "Apply to rent" form being filled in with the sample data, ending on Submit (the CAPTCHA step is done by you) |
| 4 | The dashboard list of rental applications for the test listing |
| 5 | The Open Home Hero logo and openhomehero.com.au on navy |

## ElevenLabs steps (you do these)

1. Sign in at elevenlabs.io. Start Starter only when you are ready to publish (about A$9 a month; the free plan is fine for trying voices but not for posting).
2. Pick a stock voice with an Australian accent from the voice library; listen to the first sentence before choosing.
3. Paste one scene at a time and click Generate. Download each as an MP3. Name them scene1.mp3 to scene5.mp3.
4. Regenerating a scene uses credits again. The whole script is about 600 characters, so retakes are cheap.

## Assembly

Drag the screen recording and the five MP3s into iMovie (or CapCut), line each scene up with its audio, add captions from the narration text, export. Aim for under 60 seconds.

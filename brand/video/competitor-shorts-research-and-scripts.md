## Patterns that win
1. Short: the hits are 8 to 60 seconds; the biggest is 12 seconds.
2. A rule or a secret in the first two seconds ("Never...", "The perfect... script"). The title is the hook.
3. One idea per video. Nothing tries to explain a whole product.
4. Show the surprising detail, not the feature list.
5. Agent-targeted videos about strategy or scripts beat product ads by roughly 2x (46k to 54k against 28k to 30k).
6. Software brands almost never get above 30k. A realistic first goal is 5k to 30k views, not millions.

## What we should and should not copy
- Copy: the hook-first structure, one idea, under 30 seconds, a plain close.
- Do not copy: competitor names, claims about being the "#1" or "most popular", or anything about rentals while the rental hold is on.
- No invented stats, testimonials or results. Illustrative scenes must be labelled.
- Label AI-generated footage as AI-generated in the post, as we do for the voice-over.
- Real QR code is added in editing, because AI video cannot draw a readable one.

## Short 1: "The open home mistake" (about 18 s). Format: rule/secret hook
Hook (0 to 2 s), on screen: "Stop using a clipboard at your open home."
Beats
1. Agent at the front door holds a clipboard. Close-up of a messy page, one entry crossed out. Caption: "Half of it you can't read on Monday." (No statistic is claimed; the caption describes the problem, not a number. Reword to "Some of it you can't read on Monday." if preferred.)
2. Cut to a flyer on a stand by the door with a QR code. A visitor scans it.
3. Phone screen: three taps, "Thanks, that's sent."
4. Agent's phone buzzes: "New feedback."
5. End card: "One QR code. No clipboard." / openhomehero.com.au / "14-day free trial".
Voice-over (optional, AI voice labelled): "Stop using a clipboard at your open home. Put a QR code on the flyer. Visitors scan, tap a few answers, and you get readable feedback before you've packed up."
Caption: Stop using a clipboard at your open home. Click the link in our bio for a preview. #OpenHome #RealEstateAustralia

## Short 2: "Seller calls on Monday" (about 12 s). Format: relatable pain
Hook (0 to 2 s), on screen: "Seller calls: 'So how did the open home go?'"
Beats
1. Agent at desk, phone ringing, caption "Monday 9am."
2. Agent's face: pause. Caption: "You remember six people. They had forty."
   (Illustrative numbers in a scene; the post must say "Example scene" or "Illustration".)
3. Cut: agent opens the dashboard, sorted feedback appears: "Very interested" x3, "Considering" x2, one "Ready to offer".
4. Agent: "Eleven came through. Three were very interested, one is ready to make an offer."
5. End card: "Know the answer before they ask." / openhomehero.com.au
Caption: Know the answer before the seller asks. (Illustration. Sample data.) #OpenHome #RealEstateAustralia

## Short 3: "Scan on the way out" (about 22 s). Format: short demo, the competitor ad format done better
Hook (0 to 2 s), on screen: "No app. No account. Nine seconds."
Beats
1. Visitor walks out of an open home, passes a flyer by the door.
2. Visitor lifts phone, scans the QR code. Caption: "Scan."
3. Phone screen: tap "Very interested", tap three star ratings. Caption: "Tap."
4. Thanks screen. Caption: "Done."
5. Cut to the agent's phone: "New feedback. 24 Grandview Terrace."
6. End card: "Feedback in seconds. No app. No account." / openhomehero.com.au / "14-day free trial".
Caption: Scan. Tap. Done. Click the link in our bio for a preview. #OpenHome #PropTech

## AI generation notes (for the clips)
- Style: realistic phone-shot vertical, natural daylight, Australian suburban house, no readable text in the AI footage (add all text and the QR in editing).
- Per clip prompt example: "Vertical 9:16, handheld phone footage, a visitor in casual clothes walks out of the front door of a suburban Australian house during an open home and stops at a flyer on a stand by the door, lifts their phone to scan it, natural daylight, shallow depth of field, no text visible."
- Keep each AI shot to 4 to 5 seconds; 3 to 6 shots per video.
- Edit text, QR code, phone screens and end card in our own pipeline.

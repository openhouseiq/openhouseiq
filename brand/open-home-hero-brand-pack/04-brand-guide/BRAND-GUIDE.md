# Open Home Hero brand guide

## 1. The idea

Open Home Hero helps real estate agents run their open homes and rental
inspections: a QR code gets every visitor checked in, and their feedback,
offers and rental applications land in one place. The brand should feel
**trustworthy, calm and capable**, like a well-run open home, not loud or
gimmicky. The agent is the hero; the product keeps them organised.

## 2. The name

* Write it as **Open Home Hero**: three words, capital O, H and H.
* Never "OpenHomeHero", "OHH" or "Open Home Heroes". The web address
  (openhomehero.com.au) is the only place the words run together.
* Possessive: "Open Home Hero's".
* Don't use ™ or ® until a trade mark application has been filed (ask the attorney).

## 3. The logo

**The mark** is an open roof over a serif H. The roof is *open* (a line, not a
closed shape) for "open home"; the H is for Hero. The mark sits inside a circle
safely, so it survives the round crops that social platforms apply.

**Versions**

| Use | File |
|---|---|
| Light backgrounds (white, cream) | `horizontal-for-light-backgrounds` / `stacked-for-light-backgrounds` |
| Dark backgrounds (navy, photos) | `horizontal-for-dark-backgrounds` / `stacked-for-dark-backgrounds` |
| One-colour navy | `*-navy-one-colour` |
| One-colour white | `*-white-one-colour` |
| Mark only | `mark-tile` (navy tile), `mark-on-dark`, `mark-on-light` |
| Wordmark only | `wordmark-for-light-backgrounds` / `wordmark-for-dark-backgrounds` |

**Clear space:** keep an empty margin around the logo at least as wide as the
roof of the mark is tall (about one seventh of the mark's height). Nothing else
sits inside it.

**Minimum size:** mark alone 24 px (16 px for a browser tab); horizontal logo
140 px wide or 35 mm in print; stacked logo 100 px wide.

**Do not**
* stretch, squash or rotate the logo;
* recolour it outside the palette below;
* put the cream-and-brass logo on a light background (use the light version);
* add shadows, outlines or effects;
* rebuild the lettering in another font.

## 5. Typography

* **Source Serif 4** for the logo, headlines and anything that should feel
  established. Semibold (600) for the logo and tagline, Medium (500) for headings.
* **Inter** for body copy, buttons, forms and small text. Regular (400) and Medium (500).
* Both are free on Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font Licence.

## 6. Voice

* Plain and practical, written for busy agents. Short sentences.
* Lead with what the agent gets: fewer paper sign-in sheets, every visitor
  captured, every offer in one place.
* Say "open home" for Australian audiences and "open house" for US audiences.
* Avoid hype ("revolutionary", "game-changing"). Show the result instead.

**Tagline:** Every visitor. Every offer. One QR code.

**One-line description:** Open Home Hero turns every open home into instant
visitor feedback, offers and rental applications with one QR code.

## 8. Using the files

* Upload the PNGs as they are. Do not resize them first.
* To change the tagline on a banner or post, open the matching file in
  `05-editable-svg-sources` in Figma, Canva (import), Inkscape or Illustrator.
  Text in these files is converted to outlines, so retyping needs the fonts above.
* To regenerate the whole pack after a logo change, see `brand/source/` in the
  website's code: `build_brand.py` rebuilds every file from `logo.py`.
